Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 · Last Updated: November 15, 2026

TAPX (https://xtap.vercel.app) is a personal multi-wallet expense tracking web application developed and operated by Kalp Patel. Your financial records belong strictly to you—we never sell, rent, advertise against, or share your personal or financial information with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

TAPX collects only the minimum information necessary to authenticate your account, synchronize your balances across devices, and power quick transaction logging:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google OAuth, we receive your basic profile information (display name, email address, and unique Google User ID) via standard openid, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile scopes. TAPX does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or external bank accounts.
  • User-Entered Financial Records: Wallets/accounts, initial and current balances, custom expense or income categories, optional monthly spending limits, currency preferences, transaction amounts, notes, and timestamps that you voluntarily enter into TAPX.
  • Automation & Local Storage Data: A unique, revocable Shortcut Security Token generated for your account to authenticate iPhone Back Tap / Apple Shortcuts requests, along with local browser storage (localStorage) on your device to provide instant offline-ready loading and theme preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process the information described above exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your session and isolating your workspace from other users.
  • Calculating wallet balances, inter-wallet transfers, category totals, and spending insights.
  • Validating and recording quick expense or income entries submitted through your personal Apple Shortcuts webhook URL.
  • Maintaining application uptime, security, and database integrity.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

TAPX's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, Google user data obtained through sign-in is used solely to provide and maintain user-facing authentication within TAPX and is never transferred to third-party data brokers, used for targeted advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Infrastructure & Service Providers

TAPX relies on trusted cloud infrastructure providers to host the application and store your records securely:

  • Supabase: Provides user authentication and managed PostgreSQL database hosting. All tables enforce PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) so that only your authenticated account can read or modify your records.
  • Vercel: Hosts the TAPX web interface and executes serverless API endpoints (such as /api/shortcut) over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Data Security

All network communication between your device, Vercel, and Supabase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to your data is protected by Google OAuth 2.0 session tokens and database-level Row-Level Security policies. If you ever suspect your Apple Shortcuts token has been exposed, you can immediately invalidate it and generate a new token inside Settings → Back Tap Setup.

6. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

Your financial data is retained only while your TAPX account remains active. You maintain direct control over your information at all times:

  • In-App Controls: You can edit or permanently delete individual transactions, wallets, and categories directly inside TAPX at any time. Signing out automatically clears local cached data from your browser.
  • Full Account Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your TAPX account and all associated database records at any time by emailing kalppatel132008@gmail.com from your registered email address. Deletion requests are completed within 7 days.

7. Children's Privacy

TAPX is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to TAPX or changes in legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
